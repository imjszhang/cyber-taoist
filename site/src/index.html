Explore the practice↗

An illustrative experiment, not a reported result.

The question
Will writing a short note in my own words help me use what I read?
The experiment
After each of three articles this week, write one claim, its source, and one way to use it.
The feedback
At the end of the week, check whether any note helped with a real decision or piece of work.
The revision
If the notes did not help, change the format or the question. Keep what proves useful.

Your first practice

Choose a question you face. Try one small experiment.

    Write down your view and evidence

  2. 02

    Try something you can afford to get wrong

  3. 03
